International Workshop

How Digital Decision-Support Tools Can Strengthen Urban Micro-Mobility

Online
Organised by Sustainability InnoCenter & Aalborg University

Agenda

01 Welcome and introduction
02 Initial demo of the Micro-Mobility Decision Support Tool (MDST), presented by Aalborg University
03 Discussion sessions: stakeholder feedback on the tool and potential areas for improvement
04 Breakout rooms by Living Lab city: Brașov, Osmangazi, Rethymno, Bochum, and Cologne

This online workshop invites researchers, urban planners, and mobility practitioners to explore how digital decision-support tools can guide smarter micro-mobility planning. Organised by Sustainability InnoCenter and Aalborg University as part of the PROMISE project, the session presents the Micro-Mobility Decision Support Tool (MDST) under development and its application across PROMISE living lab cities.
