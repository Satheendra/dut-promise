Mobilitix: the PROMISE Micro-mobility Decision Support Tool demo is online

Try it

The Micro-mobility Decision Support Tool (MDST), developed in Work Package 3 under the lead of Aalborg University, is now available to explore online as Mobilitix.

The interactive GIS demo lets users switch between cities, plot the shortest cycling route from any point on the map, and explore bikeability, walkability, and composite indexes alongside land use layers and reachability isochrones for 5, 10, and 15 minute travel times.
